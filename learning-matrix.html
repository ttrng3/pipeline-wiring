Seven moves for what is live: CRV/TM-01, the ELA ~40bn commit, the weekly report
1

Pre-empt the walkback. A midday intervention cuts the protective terms first, so stage the rent floor, CPI floor, guarantee and put option as later "legal-review" lines, not lead term-sheet items. The tell: a new counterparty principal introduced same-day.

CRV/TM-01"8 of 9 protective terms" survived only the structural ones
2

Distrust the 60M GMV. It runs about 2.9× the Vinh comparable, and turnover rent plus IRR scale straight off it. Model a 20 / 40 / 60M band and anchor the negotiation to the low end before any floor is signed.

CRV/TM-01Vinh comp ≈ 800 USD/sqm (~20.4M VND)
3

Below the 12% hurdle, stop negotiating the headline. A 3–5% rent bump does not rescue a sub-WACC deal. The real move is a restructure: revenue-share for fixed rent, earn-out for upfront, CAPEX pushed to the counterparty, or it is a walk.

CRV/TM-01 · ELAPM lease NPV@12% = −7.3 bn
4

Legal-gate the ELA ~40bn social-infra commit. For a binary legal risk (the Vitalia/DKGroup licence), disburse one small tranche to buy a written external Go/No-Go first; a No-Go halts the program with the rest untouched.

ELA · KSNB/QTRRtranche buys the Go/No-Go before the ~89% commit
5

Fix the weekly report before scaling anything. Signed-but-dark space is a bigger and cheaper-to-fix loss than vacancy, and occupancy conceals it. Add a leased-but-dark count and a downgrade-rate line; measure the pipeline by rent value.

ELA · TMDV · KSNB/QTRR"It is leased space that never opened."
6

Lock kill criteria before the money moves. A threshold with no named data source cannot fire, and every risk owner must be a real person who agreed, not a hypothetical role. Do this on the ELA commit and on Vitals.

KSNB/QTRR · Vitals"a threshold with no data feed cannot fire"
7

On the TM-01 transfer question: never sell the land. If transfer-at-cost is floated against a lease, set the floor at the NPV-zero price including opportunity cost, then bridge with an earn-out rather than parting with title.

CRV/TM-01transfer needs 244 bn just to reach NPV = 0
